Stake = attractiveness/10 x 15% of bankroll, x0.25 for underdog picks, x0.50 when attractiveness is 3.3 or lower (marked *). Minimum attractiveness 6.5. Total stakes per day are capped at 50% of bankroll. Polymarket payout: net profit = bet_amount x (1/price - 1) on win, -bet_amount on loss.
